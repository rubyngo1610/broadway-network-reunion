# Who works together again (strict credits)

Share of teammate pairs who share another Broadway show within 10 years. Every pair of teammates on a musical that opened 1900–2015 (6,371 pairs; a pair on several shows counts once per show). Number of pairs in brackets.

| Team size | First time together | 1 show together before | 2+ shows together before |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2–3 | 7% (745) | 22% (90) | 68% (60) |
| 4–5 | 8% (2,507) | 26% (204) | 54% (179) |
| 6–8 | 5% (1,714) | 32% (93) | 55% (69) |
| 9+ | 1% (683) | 18% (17) * | 30% (10) * |

\* fewer than 30 pairs, so the rate is uncertain. 95% intervals are in `reunion_by_team_size_history.csv`.

Roles: composers, lyricists, book writers, directors and choreographers of original productions. Source: Wikidata and Wikipedia; written by `scripts/16_partnership_table.py`.
